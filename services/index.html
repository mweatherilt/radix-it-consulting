Services

Infrastructure that stays visible and recoverable.

We help business IT teams establish monitoring, backup, security, and network practices that reduce unplanned downtime and make recovery predictable.

Monitoring & alerting

Establish coverage across servers, services, and network components so your team receives meaningful early warning. Engagements often include platforms such as Zabbix, with alert design focused on actionable thresholds.

  • Host, service, and network health checks
  • Alerting prioritized for response, not volume
  • Operational guidance so internal teams can sustain the setup

Backup & recovery

Design and validate backup processes for the systems that matter most. Work typically includes platforms such as Acronis, with restore testing treated as a required part of the process.

  • Backup coverage for critical systems and data
  • Documented restore procedures and periodic validation
  • Clear recovery expectations for ownership and leadership

Cybersecurity

Practical security support for organizations responsible for customer information — including patch discipline, configuration hardening, and visibility into infrastructure changes.

  • Foundational defenses suited to smaller IT teams
  • Patch and configuration hygiene that remains maintainable
  • Controls that protect customer data without unnecessary complexity

Network operations

Ongoing support for firewalls, switching, wireless, and related infrastructure. The goal is a stable, documented network that can be monitored and changed safely.

  • Firewall and network monitoring
  • Day-to-day operational support for configuration changes
  • Inventory and documentation of network assets and purpose
