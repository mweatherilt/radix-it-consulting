Services

Keep the environment visible, recoverable, and boring.

Most IT problems do not announce themselves. By the time someone says “email’s down,” you have already lost hours. We help you watch the stack before that call.

Monitoring & alerting

Catch issues early — before users notice and everyone scrambles. We help you watch servers, services, and the network with tools like Zabbix, then tune alerts so they mean something.

  • Host, service, and network checks that actually get looked at
  • Alerting that surfaces the warning, not the noise
  • Day-to-day guidance so a small team can run what they deploy

Backup & recovery

If you have not tested a restore lately, you do not have a backup. You have a hope. We work with tools like Acronis so recovery is a procedure, not a prayer.

  • Backup jobs that cover the systems that matter
  • Restore tests, not just “did it complete”
  • Data protection you can explain to an owner in one sentence

Cybersecurity

If your customers trust you with their information, that is the job. Practical security and compliance support — patching, hardening, and knowing who changed the last firewall rule.

  • Frontline defenses for shops that cannot staff a SOC
  • Patch and configuration hygiene that stays in place
  • Help protecting customer data without theater

Network ops

Firewalls, switching, wireless, and the rest of the stack that keeps the office on the network. Watch it. Patch it. Know what changed.

  • Firewall and network monitoring
  • Day-to-day operations so small changes do not become outages
  • A clear picture of what is on the network and why
