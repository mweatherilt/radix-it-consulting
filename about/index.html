About

Practical ops for teams that have to keep the lights on.

Radix IT Consulting is based in Clearwater, Florida. Matt Weatherilt has been doing this work since 2001.

The practice exists to help business IT departments manage and monitor their environments so they catch issues early — before email goes down and everyone scrambles.

Day to day that means monitoring, backup, security, and network operations. Tools help. Judgment matters more. We would rather leave you with a system you can run than a black box you have to call about.

If your customers trust you with their information, quiet systems are the job.
