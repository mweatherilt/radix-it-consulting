About

Operational IT support with lasting continuity.

Radix IT Consulting is based in Clearwater, Florida, and has provided business IT operations services since 2001 under the leadership of Matt Weatherilt.

The practice focuses on helping business IT departments manage and monitor their environments so issues are identified early and resolved with clear ownership.

Engagements typically cover monitoring, backup and recovery, cybersecurity, and network operations. We favor solutions your team can operate confidently — documented, maintainable, and aligned with how the business actually runs.

When customers entrust you with their information, reliable systems and disciplined operations are part of that responsibility.
